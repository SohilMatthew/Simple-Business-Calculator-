 Business Revenue & Sales Tracker

 About The App
  * Minimalist Android digital ledger designed specifically for local food shops and small businesses.
  * Replaces manual paper logs and spreadsheets with a fast, structured digital entry system.
  * Operates 100% offline, keeping sensitive financial records private on the local device.

 Use Cases
  * Daily Ledger Management: Log daily inventory investments, gross sales, and itemized customer orders in seconds.
  * Business Performance Monitoring: Monitor daily profit margins and evaluate sales trends over time.
  * Menu & Catalog Customization: Adapt daily input forms dynamically when menu items or prices change.

 Core Features
  * Dynamic Itemized Logging: Generates input fields based on active menu items to track exact unit sales per day.
  * Instant Profit Calculation: Automatically computes net daily profit by subtracting cost investments from total revenue.
  * 30-Day Financial Projections: Estimates rolling monthly profit metrics based on historical daily performance averages.
  * Growth & Trend Analytics: Displays revenue performance comparisons against prior days and weeks alongside a 7-day visual trend graph.
  * Complete Historical Log: Stores a chronological record of past financial entries with full sales breakdowns.
  * Menu Catalog Control: Toggle active items or update product lists to keep daily entry forms up to date.
 Technical Overview
  * UI Framework: Jetpack Compose (Declarative UI)
  * Local Storage: Room Database (SQLite persistence)
  * Architectural Pattern: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
